## FAQ

**When will Walmart announce its official Black Friday dates?**
Based on the last three years, expect the announcement in the last week of October — October 31 in 2023, October 28 in 2024, October 27 in 2025. Until then, all 2026 scheduling beyond November 27 itself is projection.

**Does the 8% cash back apply to everything at Walmart?**
No. Flash Deals and Extra Savings categories earn up to 8%, most general merchandise earns 3%, electronics and media earn 2%, and game consoles earn nothing. Always check the category breakdown before assuming a percentage on a big purchase.

**Do Walmart stores open on Thanksgiving?**
No. Walmart stores close on Thanksgiving and reopen at 6 a.m. local time on Black Friday, though online sales run straight through the holiday.
